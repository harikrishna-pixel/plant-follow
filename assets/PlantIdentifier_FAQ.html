1. How does the app work?

Simply take or upload a clear photo of a plant. Our AI system will analyze it and show the plant’s name, scientific classification, and care details within seconds.

2. What features are included in the free version?

The free version allows limited daily scans, access to basic plant information, and limited care tips.

3. What extra benefits do I get with Plant Identifier Premium?

  • Unlimited plant scans
  • Detailed care and disease diagnosis
  • Watering and sunlight reminders
  • Ad-free experience
  • Priority AI processing for faster results

4. How do I subscribe to Premium?

Go to the Premium section inside the app and choose your preferred plan (monthly, 6-month, or yearly). Payment will be charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase.

5. How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime from your App Store account settings:
Manage Subscription

6. Will my subscription renew automatically?

Yes, your subscription will automatically renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends.

7. What happens if I delete the app?

Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription. You need to cancel it through your Apple ID account settings.

8. Can I get a refund?

Apple handles all billing and refund requests. Please visit Apple Support to request a refund.

9. I purchased Premium, but it’s not unlocked.

Try restoring your purchase:

  1. Go to Settings → Restore Purchases inside the app.
  2. Make sure you’re logged into the same Apple ID used for the purchase.
  3. Ensure you have a stable internet connection.
If the issue persists, contact us at: plantcare@marberx.com

10. Is my data safe?

Yes. We respect your privacy and do not collect or share personal information. For more details, see our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

11. Can I use the app for non-plant objects?

No. The app is specifically designed for identifying plants and plant-related elements (flowers, leaves, trees, etc.).

12. How accurate are the results?

Our AI is trained on millions of verified plant images and achieves a very high accuracy rate. However, lighting and image clarity can affect results. For best results, take clear, well-lit photos.
